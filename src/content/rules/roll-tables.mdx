The tables in this section are designed to inspire various aspects of your Character, such as their species, archetype, and other details. Feel free to draw as much or as little inspiration from these tables as you like. Remember, rolling on a table does not mean you are obligated to use the result; it’s merely a suggestion.

To roll on a table, use the die indicated at the top of the table. Then, match the number you roll to the corresponding result in the table.

Species (d8). Roll for a species using the table below. If you roll "Mixed," roll twice more to combine the two species you get, re-rolling any additional "Mixed" results. These species are part of the base game, but additional resources may include their own roll tables; or you can create your own!
